# Geometric Aesthetics
## Volume 13 of the Geometric Series

**Subtitle:** *The Mathematical Structure of Judgment*

**Thesis:** Aesthetic judgment has geometric structure that a single scalar ("a 4-star book", "a beautiful song") cannot represent. Aesthetic evaluation is not a point on a line — it is a location in a space with dimensions, distances, directions, regimes, and curvature. When we flatten this structure into a scalar we lose information, and the information we lose is precisely the information that matters most in hard cases: which qualities are present, where uncertainty concentrates, how judgments change across genres and regimes, and where the rules discontinuously change.

## Part I — The Problem (Ch. 1–3)

- **Ch. 1 — Introduction: Why Geometry?** — Maya's manuscript. The shape of the problem. A hut, a reed, and a urinal: three fragments, three centuries, three demonstrations that aesthetic value has dimensions a scalar conceals.
- **Ch. 2 — The Failure of Scalar Aesthetics** — Recommendation systems, AI-generated art, and canon-formation as three collapses of structure into score.
- **Ch. 3 — Historical Precursors: Geometry Before Geometry** — Pythagorean ratios, Chōmei's *Hōjōki*, Zeami's *fūshi* / *hana*, Kant's form–matter split, Fechner's inner aesthetics, Birkhoff's *Aesthetic Measure*, Bense's *Informationsästhetik*.

## Part II — Foundations (Ch. 4–9)

- **Ch. 4 — Mathematical Preliminaries** — Manifolds, tangent bundles, covectors, tensors, metrics, connections, curvature, fiber bundles, stratified spaces, gauge theory.
- **Ch. 5 — The Aesthetic Manifold** — Dimensions of the perceiver's experience; local charts; the tangent space of judgment.
- **Ch. 6 — The Tensor Hierarchy** — Rank-0 scalar collapse, rank-1 directional judgment, rank-2 joint evaluation, and the higher-rank aesthetic objects that AI curators are already implicitly manipulating.
- **Ch. 7 — One Work, Five Levels** — A single novel analyzed at five tensorial ranks, showing what each rank recovers that the previous loses.
- **Ch. 8 — Stratification: Boundaries, Thresholds, Phase Transitions** — Where the rules change discontinuously (genre borders, medium switches, cultural boundaries).
- **Ch. 9 — Origin of the Aesthetic Metric** — Where the distance function comes from; perceptual covariance as learned metric; cross-cultural convergence and divergence.

## Part III — Dynamics (Ch. 10–15)

- **Ch. 10 — Aesthetic Dynamics: Parallel Transport, Style, Holonomy** — How a work changes as it moves through contexts; the holonomy group of interpretive communities.
- **Ch. 11 — Aesthetic Reasoning as Optimal Search** — A* on manifold space; planning a revision; the decision problem an artist actually solves.
- **Ch. 12 — Noether's Theorem for Aesthetics** — Symmetries of aesthetic evaluation and their conserved quantities.
- **Ch. 13 — Quantum Aesthetic Dynamics: Superposition, Measurement** — Why aesthetic judgment *changes the work judged* — measurement back-action in taste.
- **Ch. 14 — Collective Aesthetic Agency: Canon and Emergence** — How groups produce aesthetic consensus that no individual holds; canon-formation as attractor dynamics.
- **Ch. 15 — From Tensor to Judgment** — The contraction that produces the final verdict, and what it throws away.

## Part IV — Meta (Ch. 16–19)

- **Ch. 16 — Aesthetic Uncertainty and the Limits of Geometric Determinacy** — Where the theory says "don't know," and why that answer is a feature.
- **Ch. 17 — Empirical Evidence for Geometric Aesthetics** — Pre-registered, double-blind validation of the core predictions on 50,000+ Goodreads reviews, 9 cultural traditions, 3,300 years of text.
- **Ch. 18 — Geometric Aesthetics for Artificial Agents** — How an AI curator should represent aesthetic judgment; why rank-0 evaluation guarantees recommendation collapse.
- **Ch. 19 — DEME Architecture for Aesthetics** — The Democratically Governed Ethics Module Engine applied to aesthetic domains; tensorial moral + aesthetic evaluation as a single architecture.
